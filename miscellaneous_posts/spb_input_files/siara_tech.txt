 #p A new perspective on networking and self-marketing for me.

#card-title Not into social media

 #p Making myself marketable was always about my resume and posting online. The latter has been way less engaging and maintainable.

 #p Social media like Instagram, Facebook, and Reddit don't cross my mind very often, and that is no different with LinkedIn. I don't feel like scrolling or posting myself. I was always like that.

 #p Aside from YouTube, the only social platform I frequently use is WhatsApp. I feel way more willing to interact with people directly.

#card-title Networking idea

 #p Thanks to my job, I have been participating in professional events. Siará Tech Summit is the third event. There, I got to present the company to the public with my boss.

 #p While talking with visitors at my stand or being a visitor myself at other stands, I got to do a good amount of networking. People were nice and interesting. Although I am mostly an introvert and interacting with the public is not my natural inclination, I was enjoying it to some degree.

 #p That good experience made me wonder about participating in more events in the future and having a company myself, primarily in order to do more meaningful networking with relevant connections. I would not just be an I.T. professional searching for opportunities, but I would also have a brand and clients behind me.

#card-p I am still not thrilled to engage on LinkedIn a lot, but let's see if my future plans are going to implement my current ideas.
