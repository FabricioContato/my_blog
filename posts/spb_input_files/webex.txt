Kobegeens’s site has a very cool-looking theme. I really like this old-school computer aesthetic—a nice green terminal that I feel like copying into one of my projects.

The second one isn’t very flashy, but it made me aware of webmentions. That’s not a very popular topic. I mean, what is popular in tech nowadays if not AI? Webmentions are a way to notify a website when its link is posted somewhere else. They’re meant to allow inter-website communication. Some articles compare this to social media features. It’s curious to think that there’s a serious movement toward social communication outside of social media. It feels like resistance to the giants.
